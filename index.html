What it does on a job

Records the pump-out where you stand

Gallons, waste type, what you saw in the tank, the customer's signature. It works at the end of a dirt road with no bars, because everything is kept on the phone.

Knows what is on the truck

Every pickup stays on the truck's running total until you record the dump. It warns you when the gallons add up to more than the tank holds, or when a load mixes septage with grease.

One tap at the plant

Pick the receiving facility, enter its receipt number, and every pickup on that load is stamped with where and when it went. The load sheet lists each customer, town and gallons.

Prints the record

A pumping record and hauling ticket for each job: customer, tank, gallons, condition, driver, vehicle, your registration number, the facility and its permit number. Share it from the phone.

Adds up the year

Gallons by facility and waste type, by town, and by month, for a calendar year or the June-to-May year Texas uses. The numbers your yearly report asks for, without a shoebox of tickets.

Brings customers back

Each tank has a due date. Open the list, see who is overdue or coming up, and send a reminder from your own phone or email. It can suggest an interval from tank size and household size, using a published extension-service table.

What it does not do

  • It keeps records on one device. There is no office-and-truck sync yet. It suits an owner who drives the truck. A backup file moves everything to another phone or tablet, and you should save one regularly.
  • It prints one clear record and one summary layout. It does not reproduce each state's or county's own form, and it does not file anything for you. Use its totals to fill in the form your agency requires.
  • It is not dispatch or accounting software. No route planning, no invoicing, no card payments. It records the charge and how it was paid, and that is all.
  • It does not know your local rules. Ticket, copy and reporting rules differ by state and county. You set the deadlines that apply to you.

Privacy

TankDue is a record-keeping tool. It does not replace your licence conditions or what your state and county require.
